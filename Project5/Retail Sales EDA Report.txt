Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report

Project 5: Retail Sales Analysis

Dataset
File Name: retail_sales.csv


1. Introduction

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify important sales patterns, customer behavior, and business insights.


2. Data Overview

The dataset contains information related to retail transactions, including:

* Transaction ID
* Date
* Customer information
* Product or category
* Quantity
* Sales or revenue
* Region

The dataset contains numerical, categorical, and date-related features.


3. Data Cleaning

The dataset was checked for:

* Missing values
* Duplicate records
* Incorrect data types
* Invalid values
* Date formatting

The data was cleaned and prepared before performing further analysis.


4. Descriptive Statistics

The following statistical measures were calculated:

* Mean
* Median
* Standard deviation
* Minimum
* Maximum
* Total sales

These measures help understand overall retail sales performance.


5. Exploratory Data Analysis

The following analyses were performed:

* Total sales analysis
* Category-wise sales analysis
* Region-wise sales analysis
* Quantity analysis
* Customer purchasing analysis
* Sales trend over time
* Product/category performance
* Relationship between numerical features


6. Data Visualization

The following visualizations were created:

* Category-wise Sales Bar Chart
* Region-wise Sales Bar Chart
* Sales Trend Line Chart
* Sales Distribution Histogram
* Category-wise Sales Box Plot
* Correlation Heatmap


7. Sales Trend Analysis

Sales were analyzed over time to identify increasing or decreasing sales patterns.

The analysis helps understand periods with higher and lower sales performance.


8. Category Analysis

Different product categories were compared based on sales and quantity.

This helps identify high-performing and low-performing categories.


9. Regional Analysis

Sales performance was compared across different regions.

This helps identify regions that contribute more to overall sales.


10. Key Insights

* Sales performance varies across different product categories.
* Some categories contribute more to total revenue than others.
* Sales can vary across different regions.
* Time-based analysis helps identify sales trends.
* Data visualization makes important sales patterns easier to understand.


11. Business Insights

The analysis can help businesses:

* Identify high-performing categories.
* Understand regional sales performance.
* Monitor sales trends.
* Improve inventory and sales planning.
* Make data-driven business decisions.


12. Conclusion

The Retail Sales Analysis project demonstrates how Python and data analysis techniques can be used to clean, explore, visualize, and interpret retail sales data.

The analysis provides useful insights into sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product categories, and regional trends.
